I am delighted to share that [IEEE 2816-2026](https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/2816/7620/), a recommended practice for computational electromagnetics applied to the modeling and simulation of antennas, has been approved by the IEEE SA Standards Board. I participated in its development as a member of the *Computational Electromagnetics (CEM) Working Group*.
